01 — About

A developer who sweats the details

I'm a web developer who loves turning ideas into fast, accessible products people actually enjoy using. Over the past several years I've built everything from marketing sites to data-heavy dashboards, always chasing the same goal: interfaces that feel effortless on the surface and stay maintainable underneath. I care as much about a clean component API and a green test suite as I do about the pixel-perfect polish a user sees.

My work sits at the intersection of design and engineering. I prototype quickly, collaborate closely with designers and product teams, and I'm happiest shipping in small, confident increments. When I'm not writing code you'll find me reading about type, contributing to open source, or mentoring developers taking their first steps on the web.
